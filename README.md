Banking Customer & Financial Analytics

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end retail banking analytics project using PostgreSQL, Python, and Power BI.

The objective is to analyze customer behavior, transaction activity, product relationships, and credit performance to identify important customer segments, potential risks, and opportunities for improving customer relationships.

## Business Problem

A retail bank has customer, account, transaction, loan, card, and geographic data across multiple datasets.

Management wants to better understand:

- Who its customers are
- How customers use their accounts
- How transaction activity changes over time
- Which customers demonstrate high financial activity
- How banking products are used
- Where potential credit risks exist
- Where opportunities for customer engagement and cross-selling may exist

## Dataset

The project uses the Berka / PKDD'99 Czech Banking Dataset.

The dataset contains information about:

- Customers
- Accounts
- Transactions
- Loans
- Cards
- Payment orders
- Geographic districts

The dataset contains more than one million banking transactions.
